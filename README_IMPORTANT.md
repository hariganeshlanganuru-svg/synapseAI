## ⚡ THE SOLUTION: Clear Your Browser Cache

### **STEP 1**: Hard Refresh (Try This First)

On the page showing the app, press:
- **Windows**: `Ctrl + Shift + R` or `Ctrl + F5`
- **Mac**: `Cmd + Shift + R`

### **STEP 2**: Clear Browser Cache (If Step 1 Doesn't Work)

1. Press:
   - **Windows**: `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
   - **Mac**: `Cmd + Shift + Delete`

2. Select: "Cached images and files"

3. Time: "All time" or "Last hour"

4. Click: "Clear data"

5. Close browser COMPLETELY

6. Reopen and load the app

### **STEP 3**: Use Incognito Mode (If Steps 1-2 Don't Work)

1. Press:
   - **Windows**: `Ctrl + Shift + N` (Chrome/Edge)
   - **Windows**: `Ctrl + Shift + P` (Firefox)
   - **Mac**: `Cmd + Shift + N`

2. Open `app/index.html` in incognito window

3. Should show new version immediately!
