🔄 Force Refresh Required

To see all the latest changes in SynapseAI, you need to clear your browser cache and force reload the page.

Follow these steps:

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac)
  2. Select "Cached images and files"
  3. Click "Clear data" or "Clear now"
  4. Close this window
  5. Open index.html again

Or use this quick shortcut:

Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) on the main page

Open SynapseAI (Clear Cache First!)

All changes are saved to disk and permanent. You just need to clear the old cached version from your browser.
